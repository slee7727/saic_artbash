Events

Seven events, in order of due date

01 / 07

  1. 01Due2.22

    SITE/ SIGHT

    Site/Sight is a juried, proposal-based opportunity to do a site-specific installation or architectural intervention in one of the many alternate spaces in and around the LeRoy Neiman Center. Students must apply to be considered for this event.

  2. 02Due2.29

    INTER-ACTION

    A showcase of performance and sound works. This event features both solo and collaborative performance pieces, ranging from short vignettes to durational pieces without a definitive beginning or end. Showcase takes place on the opening night of ArtBash.

  3. 03Due3.07

    BLACK BOX

    Submit sound work, single channel video, GIFs, games, apps, websites, light-based works that require dark space, multichannel video, projection/3D mapping, or works that exist in the real but have a virtual component. These works are exhibited only during the opening night of ArtBash.

  4. 04Due3.07–08

    BOOK ARTS

    Book Arts is a library that runs the duration of the ArtBash exhibition. A book, mixtape, a zine, a set of custom playing cards, or a slide carousel could easily work within these parameters.

  5. 05Due3.09

    OPEN CALL

    A selection of art and design works for the ArtBash exhibition. Students can submit ONE exhibition-quality and ready 2D or 3D work to be considered by the jury. Artworks must be dropped off at the Neiman Center.

  6. 06Due3.21

    48.24.12

    The 48.24.12 Video Contest (48 hours - 24 frames / second - 12 minute video) is a team-based, challenge based, video competition followed by a juried screening. Students must register to participate in this competition.

  7. 07Due3.24

    LATE BASH

    A selection of art and design works for the ArtBash exhibition. Students can submit ONE exhibition-quality and ready 2D or 3D work to be considered by the jury. Artworks must be dropped off at the Neiman Center.
